Project Story & Academic Motivation

Traditional retail banking has historically relied on static linear scorecards that underperform on unbanked or thin-file populations, while modern black-box ensemble methods risk perpetuating historical discrimination and opaque decisioning.

RiskLens addresses this tension by applying transparent, mathematically rigorous machine learning to sovereign and consumer default datasets. We argue that high predictive accuracy and total institutional explainability are not mutually exclusive. Through granular local Shapley additive explanations (SHAP) and strict Neyman-Pearson utility calibration, underwriter teams can audit model bias before deploying cutoffs into active production pipelines.

The Transparency Thesis

"An uncalibrated probability in retail lending is not merely poor engineering; it creates systemic balance sheet fragility and inequitable market rationing."

Data Ingestion & Cleaning Mechanics

science
Missingness Handling Numerical columns exceeding 65% nulls are pruned; remaining missing continuous covariates undergo Iterative Imputer (MICE) using predictive mean matching.
Outlier Winsorization Unrealistic values in applicant credit limits and total income are bounded at the 99.5th percentile to protect gradient boosts from catastrophic split inflation.
Out-of-Time Splitting 70% train / 15% calibration / 15% holdout test partitioned temporally to guarantee zero future-leakage into retrospective underwriting models.

Known Limitations & Ethical Considerations

pie_chart

Class Imbalance Dynamics

With defaults comprising only ~8% of total records, unweighted maximum likelihood algorithms will optimize solely for majority accuracy. RiskLens counters this via Focal Loss and precision-recall PR-AUC threshold scoring instead of misleading ROC curves.

trending_down

Macroeconomic Sensitivity

The underlying data reflects a single economic cycle. A sudden monetary policy rate shock or stagflationary environment would shift baseline hazard ratios. Stress testing coefficients are essential prior to institutional deployment.

shield_with_heart

Proxy Variable Bias

Even when explicit protected attributes (e.g., gender, ethnicity) are purged, residential ZIP codes and occupation classes can encode historical marginalization. Our governance suite mandates Four-Fifths rule testing across synthetic cohort segments.
